The Archive

Every memory is a fragment of something that once existed. Some are vivid. Others are incomplete. Some belong to places that no longer exist, or people we can no longer reach.

These are traces of lives, collected in one place.

MEMORY / 0001

The Smell of Rain

I remember sitting on the front porch during summer storms. The pavement would get dark, and the air smelled different. I don't remember the house very clearly anymore, but I remember that smell.

Childhood Summer Sense of Smell
MEMORY / 0002

A Place That Disappeared

There was a little restaurant where my family used to eat. I can picture the windows and remember the sound of dishes, but I cannot remember its name. The building is gone now.

Family Place Disappearance
MEMORY / 0003

The Bicycle

I remember learning to ride a bicycle. I remember the feeling of falling, then suddenly realizing nobody was holding on. I don't remember who was there.

Childhood Object Movement
MEMORY / 0004

A Voice I Almost Remember

I can remember the sound of someone's voice, but not the exact words. Sometimes I think I can hear it clearly. Other times I wonder if I have changed it in my memory.
