THE LIMITS OF THE CENSUS

What the Numbers Cannot Tell Us

A census is never a complete picture of humanity. It only reflects the people who participated, what they were asked, what they chose to share, and the categories used to organize their responses.

More memories of childhood do not necessarily mean childhood is more important. More memories of one place do not mean that place has more history. What is absent from this archive may still exist in countless lives.
Absence from the archive is not absence from the world.
